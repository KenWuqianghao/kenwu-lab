# Third-party material

No third-party source code, data or figures are copied into this folder.

- The definitions in `lean/`, the checkers and the paper follow X. Hou and L. Tan, *Tight lower bound for the spectral radius of connected graphs with given matching number*, [arXiv:2607.11061v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11061v2). The paper quotes Construction 9 and the question of its Section 3 word for word, with citation.
- The Lean proof uses [Lean](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4) and [Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4) (Apache-2.0). They are not redistributed here.
- The Python checks use [NetworkX](https://networkx.org) (BSD-3-Clause) and [NumPy](https://numpy.org) (BSD-3-Clause). `checks/requirements.txt` pins the versions. They are not redistributed here.
- The paper uses LaTeX with the packages listed in `paper/paper.tex`. They are not redistributed here.
