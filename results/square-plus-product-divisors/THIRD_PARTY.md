# Third-party material

No third-party source code, data or figures are copied into this folder.

- The statement of Conjecture 7 is quoted word for word, with citation, from S. Jirattikansakul, T. Kittipassorn, K. Kongsiri, N. Moonwichit, K. Sriamorn, *Congruences via Partitions with Exactly Two Part Sizes*, [arXiv:2604.25394v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25394). The paper also restates their Theorem 4 with citation.
- The formula of Zagier's involution follows D. Zagier, Amer. Math. Monthly 97 (1990), 144, [doi:10.2307/2323918](https://doi.org/10.2307/2323918). The Lean code and the proof text are our own.
- The Lean proof uses [Lean](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4) and [Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4) (Apache-2.0). They are not redistributed here.
- The sieve uses [numpy](https://numpy.org) (BSD-3-Clause), installed by `checks/requirements.txt`. It is not redistributed here.
- The paper uses LaTeX with the packages listed in `paper/paper.tex`. They are not redistributed here.
