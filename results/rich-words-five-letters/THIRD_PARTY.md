# Third-party material

No third-party code, data or figures are included in this folder. The programs, the
saved outputs, the figure and the paper were produced for this result.

The paper and the README quote one passage of L. Dvořáková, L. Mol and E. Pelantová,
*An automated approach to structure theorems for minimally repetitive rich words*,
arXiv:2611.13676v1 (2026), Section 8, item 1, and restate its Conjecture 1.1. The quotation
is marked and attributed. The cited theorems of Dolce, Dvořáková and Pelantová
(Theoret. Comput. Sci. 939 (2023)), Dvořáková and Pelantová (Electron. J. Combin. 33(2)
(2026), #P2.15) and Dvořáková, Klouda and Pelantová (European J. Combin. 126 (2025)) are
restated in our words, with references. The other cited works are bibliographic
references.

The programs import SymPy (BSD-3-Clause), mpmath (BSD-3-Clause) and NumPy
(BSD-3-Clause) at the versions in `checks/requirements.txt`; these packages are installed
from PyPI and are not included. `checks/draw_figure.py` uses Matplotlib, which is not
needed for the checks.
