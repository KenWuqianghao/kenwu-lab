# Third-party material

This folder contains no third-party code, data or figures.

## Lean and Mathlib

The proof uses [Lean](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4) and
[Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4), distributed under
Apache-2.0. Lake downloads them with the pinned versions (Lean v4.36.0, Mathlib tag
v4.36.0); their source trees and licenses are not redistributed in this folder.

## Quoted text

`README.md`, `paper/paper.tex` and the Lean doc comments quote the statement of
Conjecture 10 and short phrases from K. I. Encz, M. Mastrolilli and E. Vercesi,
"Integrality gap preserving reductions", arXiv:2609.19967v1 (2026), with citation.
