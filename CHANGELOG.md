# Changelog

Each result is versioned on its own and tagged `<slug>-vN`.

## rich-words-five-letters v1 (2026-11-27)

- A morphism π from 9 letters to 5 letters makes π(u_9) rich with critical exponent E*(u_9) ≈ 2.0592854, so the repetition threshold of 5-ary rich words is at most E*(u_9).
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580785](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580785)

## earth-rotation-quake-forecast v1 (2026-11-27)

- ComCat lists 66 M≥7 earthquakes in 2018-2022, 1.04 times the 1900-2017 rate, so a pre-registered score of the 2017 Bilham-Bendick Earth-rotation forecast (+25-30%) is inconclusive; a held-out test of its five-year lag gives no support but has only 17% power.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580744](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580744)

## lunar-phase-us-precipitation v1 (2026-11-27)

- A reported lunar-phase signal in US daily precipitation (Cerveny et al. 2010) does not replicate in held-out 2009-2026 data; in 1895-2008 its national statistic is not significant under a null that keeps the correlation between stations, and its station rate is at the 0.05 boundary.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580728](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580728)

## personal-best-quitting-lichess v1 (2026-11-27)

- On Lichess blitz 2013-2015, the jump in quitting at a new personal-best rating is +0.51 pp (95% CI -0.38 to +1.39); the 4.5 pp that Anderson and Green found on FICS is outside the interval, and the same method detects the round-number effect.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580717](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580717)

## ztf-periodic-quasars-holdout v1 (2026-11-27)

- The sinusoids of the 86 ZTF periodic-quasar candidates of Chen et al. (2024), frozen on DR11 data, do not predict the next 3.6 years of ZTF data better than red noise or than unselected quasars; none of the three named candidates is confirmed.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580702](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580702)

## morse-link-2adic-conjecture v1 (2026-11-27)

- Gao and Gu's Conjecture 4.12 on the 2-adic valuations of weighted Morse link numbers is false for k = 2, and Lean checks it; the failure comes from n = 3, and the version for n ≥ 4 stays open.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24579593](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24579593)

## ternary-legendre-pair-35 v1 (2026-11-25)

- A ternary Legendre pair of length 35 exists; this settles one of the two smallest open cases of a conjecture of Kotsireas, Liu and Winterhof, and Lean checks it.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24531165](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24531165)

## metcalf-ma-time-error v4 (2026-11-25)

- The DASCH times of Metcalf's 1905-1913 plates read his local mean time as sidereal time. At the corrected times, a faint source is at Pluto's position on four plates.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24523190](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24523190)
