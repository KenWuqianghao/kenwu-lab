# Changelog

Each result is versioned on its own and tagged `<slug>-vN`.

## half-integral-configuration-lp v1 (2026-11-27)

- Conjecture 10 of Encz, Mastrolilli and Vercesi is false: a restricted assignment instance with 12 machines, 20 jobs and LP(P) = 3 has no half-integral point in CLP(P, LP(P)), by a count modulo 3; Lean checks the disproof from the definitions.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24600794](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24600794)

## ganymed-1898-harvard-plates v1 (2026-11-27)

- A pre-registered search of public Harvard plate scans finds the near-Earth asteroid (1036) Ganymed on plates of 1898-1899, 26 years before its 1924 discovery; a weaker signal of the Jupiter Trojan (624) Hektor on Heidelberg plates of 1901 is a candidate only.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24600765](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24600765)

## applause-site-time-errors v1 (2026-11-27)

- Four APPLAUSE DR4 site records have longitude and latitude exchanged, and the Vatican archive times are local sidereal time stored as UT, which puts 40.8% of its exposures in daylight.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24585269](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24585269)

## pell-automatic-repetition-threshold v1 (2026-11-27)

- The repetition threshold of the binary Pell-automatic sequences is 7/3; this settles the Pell case of a remark of Allouche, Rampersad and Shallit, with a 4-state squarefree Pell automaton that Walnut and a second decision procedure check.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580907](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580907)

## matching-number-minimal-spectral v1 (2026-11-27)

- Hou and Tan's Construction 9 gives no tree of order 2k for k ≥ 3, so their question whether every spectrally minimal graph with matching number k lies in T'_{n,k} has a negative answer at the boundary n = 2k; Lean checks the degree count, and the range n ≥ 2k+1 stays open.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580879](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580879)

## square-plus-product-divisors v1 (2026-11-27)

- The sum of σ0(N − k²) over 1 ≤ k < √N is divisible by 4 for every N ≡ 6 (mod 8); this proves Conjecture 7 of Jirattikansakul et al. (N = 16n + 6) with an elementary proof by involutions, and Lean checks it.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580854](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580854)

## rationality-ngram-random-placebo v1 (2026-11-27)

- In a pre-registered placebo test on Google Books English, random word pairs matched in frequency, part of speech and fiction skew show the 1980 turn of the rationality/intuition word ratio as often as the original words (p = 0.126), so the test does not show that the turn is specific to these words beyond their register.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580831](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580831)

## chess-opening-zipf-lichess v1 (2026-11-27)

- On 44.8 million Lichess games the pooled opening-weight exponent is 2.09, as Blasius and Tönjes predicted, but their finite-size laws fail: alpha_d crosses 2 at depth 7.4, not about 18.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580807](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580807)

## rich-words-five-letters v1 (2026-11-27)

- A morphism π from 9 letters to 5 letters makes π(u_9) rich with critical exponent E*(u_9) ≈ 2.0592854, so the repetition threshold of 5-ary rich words is at most E*(u_9).
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580785](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580785)

## earth-rotation-quake-forecast v1 (2026-11-27)

- ComCat lists 66 M≥7 earthquakes in 2018-2022, 1.04 times the 1900-2017 rate, so a pre-registered score of the 2017 Bilham-Bendick Earth-rotation forecast (+25-30%) is inconclusive; a held-out test of its five-year lag gives no support but has only 17% power.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580744](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580744)

## lunar-phase-us-precipitation v1 (2026-11-27)

- A reported lunar-phase signal in US daily precipitation (Cerveny et al. 2010) does not replicate in held-out 2009-2026 data; in 1895-2008 its national statistic is not significant under a null that keeps the correlation between stations, and its station rate is at the 0.05 boundary.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580728](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580728)

## personal-best-quitting-lichess v1 (2026-11-27)

- On Lichess blitz 2013-2015, the jump in quitting at a new personal-best rating is +0.51 pp (95% CI -0.38 to +1.39); the 4.5 pp that Anderson and Green found on FICS is outside the interval, and the same method detects the round-number effect.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580717](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580717)

## ztf-periodic-quasars-holdout v1 (2026-11-27)

- The sinusoids of the 86 ZTF periodic-quasar candidates of Chen et al. (2024), frozen on DR11 data, do not predict the next 3.6 years of ZTF data better than red noise or than unselected quasars; none of the three named candidates is confirmed.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24580702](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24580702)

## morse-link-2adic-conjecture v1 (2026-11-27)

- Gao and Gu's Conjecture 4.12 on the 2-adic valuations of weighted Morse link numbers is false for k = 2, and Lean checks it; the failure comes from n = 3, and the version for n ≥ 4 stays open.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24579593](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24579593)

## ternary-legendre-pair-35 v1 (2026-11-25)

- A ternary Legendre pair of length 35 exists; this settles one of the two smallest open cases of a conjecture of Kotsireas, Liu and Winterhof, and Lean checks it.
- Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24531165](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24531165)

## metcalf-ma-time-error v4 (2026-11-25)

- The DASCH times of Metcalf's 1905-1913 plates read his local mean time as sidereal time. At the corrected times, a faint source is at Pluto's position on four plates.
